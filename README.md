# 🛒 Shop Performance Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyses the performance of an online shop selling electronics, accessories, wearables, home office products, stationery, and gaming products.

The goal of the analysis is to understand the shop's performance between **January 2024 and June 2026** and identify useful business insights from the data.

The analysis was completed using **Python, Pandas, and Matplotlib**.

---

## 🎯 Business Questions

The analysis focuses on questions such as:

* How much revenue did the shop generate?
* How many orders were placed?
* What is the average order value?
* Which products generate the most revenue?
* Which cities generate the most revenue?
* Which customer segments contribute the most revenue?
* How does revenue change over time?
* What recommendations can be made to improve business performance?

---

## 🔗 Data Combining

The four datasets were combined using common ID columns.

* Unit price

### Orders + Customers

Joined using:

`CustomerID`

* Customer age

### Orders + Payments

`OrderID`

* Payment date

## 🧮 Calculated Fields

A revenue column was created using:

```python
Revenue = Quantity × UnitPrice × (1 - Discount)
```

Example:

```python
df['Revenue'] = (
    df['Quantity'] *
    df['UnitPrice'] *
    (1 - df['Discount'])
)
```

Additional fields were created for analysing:

* Year
* Month
* Customer age brackets
* Customer segments
* Product performance
* City performance

 💡 Business Insights

The analysis shows that:

---

 📌 Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the business can consider:

### 1. Focus on high-revenue products

Monitor high-performing products and ensure that popular products remain available to customers.

### 2. Investigate city-level performance

Compare high- and low-performing cities to understand differences in customer demand and identify opportunities for growth.

### 3. Develop customer-segment strategies

Use customer segments to create targeted offers and encourage new customers to become repeat customers.
